Billiard Recommendations

A collection of billiard recommendations. Free, open source, no download or registration required.

Three-cushion billiards

A nicely tuned physics model that gives you the feeling of playing on a quality table. Play it and feel the quality.

Sagu

This Korean variant mixes libre and three cushion. Enjoy the synthesised scorer voice as you accumulate high runs.

Libre

Just make cannons . Sounds easy, but it really isn't.

Snooker

The staple of British billiards. With its huge table , snooker is a test of accuracy and positional play. The challenge is to make a high break.

Nineball

Ride your luck on the break, and once you get started, don't miss.

Eightball

Popular in UK pubs but more refined on American tables. Enjoy the weight of the balls and the spin that carries.
